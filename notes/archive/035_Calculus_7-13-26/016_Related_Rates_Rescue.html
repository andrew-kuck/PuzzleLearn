4 Solve the missing rate

The bottom moves away from the wall at dx/dt=0.80 m/s. Run to the target moment, then adjust dy/dt to stabilize the ladder.

x = 3.00 m
y = 4.00 m

dx/dt = 0.80 m/s
Top velocity dy/dt -0.20 m/s
Rigidity: 0%
If dy/dt is wrong, the ghost ladder stretches or breaks. If it is right, the ladder remains rigid.
Solution:
2x(dx/dt) + 2y(dy/dt) = 0
dy/dt = -(x/y)(dx/dt)
dy/dt = -(3/4)(0.80) = -0.60 m/s
