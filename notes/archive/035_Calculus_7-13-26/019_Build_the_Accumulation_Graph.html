Build the Accumulation Graph

Restore the terrain map: the top graph gives the slope/rate f(x). Your job is to build the elevation/accumulation graph F(x) = F(a) + ∫ax f(t) dt.

How to play

  • Drag the blue control points on the bottom graph to build F(x).
  • The orange scanner shows the current x-value. The shaded area above/below the x-axis on the top graph is the accumulated signed area.
  • If f(x) > 0, your F graph should increase. If f(x) < 0, it should decrease. If f(x) = 0, F has a flat tangent or local extremum.
  • Use Add Point and Remove Point to shape the path. Points snap near important x-values such as zeroes of f.
