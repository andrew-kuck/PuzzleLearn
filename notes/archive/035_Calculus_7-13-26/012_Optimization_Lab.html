Derivative hint
For a sheet with side length L, V(x)=x(L−2x)². One useful factored derivative is V′(x)=(L−2x)(L−6x). Inside the physical domain 0<x<L/2, the factor L−2x is positive, so the sign change is controlled by L−6x.
