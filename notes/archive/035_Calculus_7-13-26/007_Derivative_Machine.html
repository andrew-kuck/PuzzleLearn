How validation works

Your block machine is converted into an expression tree and compared with the true derivative at several hidden x-values. The x-values are hidden so the goal is structure, not guesswork. Sneaky, but fair.

Derivative workspace

Drag blocks into the slots, or click a block and then click a slot. Blue blocks usually mean “copy unchanged,” orange blocks mean “differentiate,” green blocks mean “rule/connective,” and red blocks are common tempting errors.
